Kcal Controller

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Kcal Controller is an AI-assisted food and calorie management application. It helps users estimate calories and macronutrients from food photographs, record meals, monitor body weight, and review nutrition and weight-management progress.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, processed, and protected when you use Kcal Controller.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the application may process the following information:

  • Account identifiers, such as an email address or telephone number.
  • Profile information, including age, sex, height, current weight, and target weight.
  • Food photographs submitted for analysis.
  • Estimated calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, and portion information.
  • Meal records, weight history, and progress summaries.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

3. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

  • Provide the application's core functions.
  • Analyze food photographs using AI.
  • Estimate nutritional values and meal portions.
  • Calculate daily calorie targets and progress.
  • Maintain meal and weight records.
  • Provide personalized nutrition suggestions.
  • Respond to support and privacy inquiries.
  • Maintain service reliability and security.

4. AI Food Analysis

When you submit a food photograph, the application may transmit the image and related request data to its backend and an external AI service for analysis.

The English version uses OpenAI technology for AI-assisted food recognition and nutritional estimation.

AI results are estimates and may be inaccurate. Users should review food names, portions, and nutritional values before recording a meal.

5. Service Providers

Kcal Controller uses external technology providers to support application functionality. These may include:

  • OpenAI, for AI-assisted food analysis.
  • Render, for application backend hosting.

Information necessary to deliver these services may be processed by the relevant provider, subject to its applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. Storage and Retention

Application information may be stored on your device and on systems supporting the service.

Information is retained as necessary to operate the service, fulfill legitimate operational needs, or comply with applicable legal obligations. Retention may vary by information type and service provider.

7. Data Security

We aim to protect personal information through appropriate technical and organizational measures. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may request information about your personal data, request correction or deletion, or ask questions about its processing.

Requests can be sent to the contact email below. We may need to verify that a request comes from the relevant account holder before taking action. Applicable rights depend on your location and law.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting the support email below.

Deletion requests will be handled subject to applicable legal requirements and any information that must legitimately be retained.

10. Age Requirements and Children's Privacy

Kcal Controller is currently intended only for adults aged 18 and older.

The application's calorie and weight-management methods are designed for adults. Children and teenagers have different nutritional needs because they are still growing and developing. To provide age-appropriate guidance, this version is not available to users under 18.

We hope to develop a dedicated Teen Mode in a future version.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If we become aware that such information has been collected, please contact us at kcalcontroller.support@gmail.com so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

11. International Data Processing

The application may use service providers operating in different countries. As a result, information may be processed outside your country of residence, subject to applicable legal requirements.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application, its service providers, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
